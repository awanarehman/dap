DAP (Daily Action Plan): privacy policy

Last updated 15 October 2026

DAP is a personal task planner written for and used by its owner. This page explains what it does with data.

What the app keeps

Your tasks, notes, reminders and settings are kept on your own devices: in your browser's storage and an optional data folder on your PC, and in the app's own storage on your phone. Nothing is sent to the app's author or to any server run by the author.

Google Drive

If you switch on sync, DAP asks Google for the drive.file permission only. With it, DAP can see and change only the files it created itself, in a folder named "Daily Action Plan" in your Google Drive. It cannot see any other file in your Drive. These files hold a copy of your plan so your PC and phone stay in step. DAP does not read your email, contacts or other Google data, and it never sees your Google password.

You can stop syncing at any time with Disconnect in DAP's Settings, and you can remove DAP's access completely from your Google Account (Security → Third-party apps with account access). You can delete the "Daily Action Plan" folder from your Drive whenever you like.

Sharing

DAP does not share, sell or transfer any data to anyone. It shows no advertising and contains no analytics or tracking.
